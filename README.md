Entry for the [Texts From Your Future Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-texts-from-your-future-self-dd44595e) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun): one funny text message, under 200 characters, that your future self sends back to you — funny by itself, with no setup and no context, just the message as it would arrive.

## The text

> it's me, you, from 2031. do NOT throw away that box of cables. you'll need exactly one of them, and you will cry.

113 characters. The joke is the most universal drawer in every house: a box of cables nobody dares throw away, and the future self confirming the paranoia was justified — exactly one cable, someday, and tears.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the text to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline. It asserts the entry is 1–200 characters, a single printable line, not only whitespace, with no leading or trailing whitespace — the exact gate the bounty scores.

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
